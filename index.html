01 · INPUTS

Payoff table

Enter payoffs for each alternative under every state of nature. Higher values are better.

Probabilities are percentages. Their total must equal 100% for EMV/Bayes calculations. Payoffs may be positive or negative.

02 · SUMMARY

Alternative statistics

Statistics are calculated from the complete original table.

03 · DECISION CRITERIA

Compare the four criteria

Each criterion independently evaluates every original alternative.

04 · STAGED FILTERING

Filter alternatives step by step

Each stage keeps the best score among the alternatives that survived the previous stage. All ties at the cutoff survive.

Filtering flow diagram

Green nodes survive each stage. Red crossed-out nodes are eliminated at that stage. Arrows show the survivor path.
